About Damiano Cerrone

Damiano Cerrone is an urbanist and educator. He is the Co-Founder of SPIN Unit, an urban research and innovation practice, and Co-Founder of CoPlanAI, a platform for collaborative urban planning, and is solving wicked problems at BAITT. Earlier, he co-founded UrbanistAI and worked at Demos Helsinki. His academic projects at Tampere University and the Estonian Academy of Arts focus on urban research and policy design for both local and national governments. He lectures on urban studies and AI at universities in Finland and abroad.
