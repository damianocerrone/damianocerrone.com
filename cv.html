Damiano Cerrone

Urbanist · co-founder of CoPlan AI and SPIN Unit
damianocerrone.com

Damiano is an urbanist and cofounder of CoPlan AI, a collaborative planning platform, and SPIN Unit, an urban research and innovation practice. He conducts research on multispecies planning at Tampere University and the Estonian Academy of Arts, focusing on urban research and policy design for both local and national governments. In addition, he teaches and collaborates with academic institutions in Europe and internationally.
